# BL-016: Softer chain colours

## Metadata
- **Epic**: Backlog
- **Status**: `completed`
- **Priority**: `medium`
- **Effort**: `M` (1-3 hours)
- **Dependencies**: BL-009 (balanced palettes), BL-014 (calm neutral theme)

## Context

Owner feedback (2026-11-16), after approving the calm neutral theme of BL-014 (dark
`#16171a` / `#1f2125`, light `#f4f4f6` / white, accent `#8ab4f8`): "Soften chain colours too."
The BL-009 palettes were bright (OKLab chroma up to 0.15) and, in the light theme, pale fills
stood out from the near-white empty squares by only 1.9:1.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Every chain in every shipped ruleset (default "Grand Hotels", valley "Acquihire", mini
      "Harborline") has lower or equal chroma than before, with its hue kept within 14 degrees
      (gold, rose, teal, copper, sage, blue, mauve stay recognisable).
- [x] Label contrast on every fill at least 4.5:1 (`readableText`).
- [x] Chains stay apart from each other for normal vision and simulated protan / deutan /
      tritan vision (the existing thresholds of the palette test, unchanged) and within the
      existing lightness band.
- [x] New: every fill at least 3:1 against the dark theme's empty square and at least 2.5:1
      against the light theme's (was 1.9:1); mini reaches 3:1 in both.
- [x] New: no chain colour can be taken for the grey of a tile in no chain (`--chain-orphan`),
      in either theme, for any of the four visions.
- [x] Frozen-colour test, frozen pricing data, the frontend fallback ruleset, docs and the
      generated wire / transcript fixtures updated (snapshot fixtures carry no colours and
      regenerate byte-identical).
- [x] Before / after screenshots at 390x675, dark and light.

## Design decisions

- **One palette for both themes.** There is no per-theme colour mechanism (a ruleset gives
  each chain one colour), and adding one was not needed: mid-lightness fills serve both boards.
- **Why not 3:1 against the light square for default and valley.** 3:1 against `#fbfbfc`
  means relative luminance at most 0.29, which with dark labels leaves a narrow lightness
  window; seven lower-chroma hues in that window cannot also stay apart under the simulated
  deficiencies and away from the orphan grey. Distinguishability won; the light-theme floor is
  2.5:1 (up from 1.9:1), and every chain region also has its abbreviation.
- **Search, then eye.** Candidates came from a constrained search in OKLCh (hue within 14
  degrees, chroma never above the old value and at most 0.115 for default / 0.13 for valley,
  label and square contrast, colour-vision distances, orphan distance), then were judged on
  swatches and on real screens.
