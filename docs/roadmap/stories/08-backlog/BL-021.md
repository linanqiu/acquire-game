# BL-021: Trading that stays put: nothing moves under the player, an offer composed in place with what it would do

## Metadata
- **Epic**: Backlog
- **Status**: `completed`
- **Priority**: `high`
- **Effort**: `L` (3+ hours)
- **Dependencies**: BL-012 (public offers), BL-019 (the page is the one scroller), LO-010 (position-aware trade valuation)

## Context

Owner feedback (2026-11-21, iPhone, about 390x675, dark, against bots and LLM seats, trading a
lot): "The trading UI is pretty bad and keeps scrolling up and down."

Two parts: find what moves the page and the tab while the player trades (measured, on real
servers), then rebuild the trading UI around what a player wants to do: "I want Bob's 2
Sandpiper; what would he take?"

## 1. What kept scrolling up and down (measured)

Real servers (`GAME_TEST_MODE=1 GAME_SEED=2`), Chromium with phone emulation (390x675,
`isMobile`, `hasTouch`), the Trades tab open, the other seats driven to act.

| What happened at the table | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| An offer is made to me while I read the Trades tab (page scrolled to 109) | Board tab, scrollY 0 | Trades tab, scrollY 109 |
| That offer is withdrawn (page scrolled to 150) | Trades tab, scrollY 0 | Trades tab, scrollY 150 |
| My place-tile turn starts (page scrolled to 150) | Board tab, scrollY 0 | Trades tab, scrollY 150 |
| The same events with the trade builder open | the tab changed under the sheet; closing it left me on the Board tab | Trades tab, the draft and the send button where they were |
| Composing one offer (six chains a side) | a sheet with 869px of rows in a 523px box that scrolled inside itself: down to "You want", back up to the players | one screen: the page is 675px high, nothing to scroll |
| Live game, five bots: a merger starting while I read the Trades tab | Board tab, scrollY 0 | Trades tab, where I was |

Causes, each reproduced and fixed:

1. **`GameLayout` scrolled the page to the top whenever the decision-event signature changed**,
   on any tab: a key added (an offer for me, a merger) or removed (an offer withdrawn or
   answered). It now does so only when a tab opens, or while the table panel, where those events
   are shown, is the tab on screen.
2. **`useGameTabs` switched tabs for my place-tile turn and for every new event** unless the chat
   input was in use. Being in the middle of a trade now counts the same way
   (`lib/gameTabs.ts: isBusyTrading`: the composer is on screen, or the Trades tab is open and
   scrolled): no switch; the Board tab gets its marker, the Trades tab its count, and the phase
   bar is the way to the decision ("PLACE A TILE · BOARD →").
3. **The builder was a scroll box of its own** (`Modal` sheet, rows in an `overflow-y: auto`
   container over a pinned footer). With more than three chains a side every offer meant
   scrolling inside it. The composer is now part of the Trades tab and fits the screen (below).
Checked and not a cause: input zoom on iOS (text fields are already 16px on touch devices); the
builder was not remounted by game states (its key only changes when it is opened; a draft of
"Dev, 1 Sandpiper, $700" survived every event above); opening the builder did not move the page
(the jump to 0 seen in the first trace was the test driver scrolling the button out from under
the header); the chat's "stay at the foot" logic only runs while the Chat tab is open; the old
offers list only grew at its foot (a new offer of mine went to the top, by my own action).

One thing kept from happening in the new list: offers for me are listed first, which would put
a new one above the card under the thumb. So the list is put in order when the player opens the
tab, and while it is open a new offer is added at its foot (the tab's badge counts it).

Not reproducible here: how iOS moves a `position: fixed` sheet when the keyboard opens for the
cash fields (no WebKit in this environment). It no longer applies: there is no sheet, the cash
has − / + buttons and one-tap amounts so the keyboard is optional, and a typed field is an
ordinary field in the page.

Rule now tested (`screens/trade-scroll.spec.ts`, fails before the fix): while the player
composes an offer or has scrolled the Trades tab, nothing but their own action moves the page
or changes the tab.

## 2. The trading UI

| Flow | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Start an offer | PROPOSE TRADE opened a full-screen sheet; pick a player; find the chain in a list of all my chains, then scroll to all of theirs | **The holdings table is the entry** (`TradeStart`): one row per opponent and mine, a column per chain anyone holds, the players table's majority / minority marks. Every name and number is a button. "I want Bob's Sandpiper" is one tap on Bob's Sandpiper: the composer opens on Bob with one Sandpiper asked. A tap on a number in my row starts an offer that gives it |
| Compose | A sheet: players, YOU OFFER (a line with a stepper per chain I hold), YOU WANT (the same for them), a footer in words | **In place on the Trades tab** (`TradeBuilder`): players on one line; **You give \| You get** side by side: a chip per chain that side holds (ruleset order; a tap adds one share; the chips never change place), cash with − / + in $100 steps and a typed amount, then one stepper line per chain that is in the deal. Both sides valued at list prices in the column headings. While composing the tab shows the composer alone, so nothing above it can grow |
| What it would do | "You give 2 Meridian $1,600 / You get 1 Marlowe + $300 $900" | Over the send button, from the server (`trade_preview`): **every change of first and second place, in words by chain** ("You take 1st · Dev drops to 2nd", third players included: "Cara ties for 1st"), or "No change of 1st or 2nd place"; **EVEN AT LIST** sets the cash that balances the two sides; and **for a rules bot what it would answer**: "Bob (bot) would accept", "would refuse: about $300 short" with **ADD $300**, or "would refuse, even for all your cash". For an LLM or a human seat: the effects only. These lines and buttons keep their room whether or not they have something to say (EVEN AT LIST and ADD are switched off, not removed), so the server's answer, arriving a moment after a tap, never moves SEND |
| Validation | Errors after pressing PROPOSE ("Select a trade partner", "You must offer something", "You must request something") | Said in place before anything is pressed ("Choose who to offer to", "Add what you give or what you get") with SEND disabled; what the engine would refuse (the limit of open offers, shares frozen by a merger) is shown as its own words before the offer is sent |
| Send | PROPOSE TRADE in the sheet's footer | **CANCEL \| SEND TO BOB**, 48px, pinned over the rack and the tab bar while the page scrolls; "SENDING…" until the server confirms; the composer closes on the confirmation and the offer is a card that says "waiting" |
| An offer for me | A card in the table panel with REJECT / ACCEPT; the Trades tab listed it with ANSWER, which jumped to the Board tab | The same card in both places, answered in place: what I give and get, **what it does to first and second place**, and **REJECT \| COUNTER \| ACCEPT** (44px, the card marked as mine to answer) |
| Counter-offer | (none) | COUNTER opens the composer on the same deal seen from my side (I give what they asked, I get what they offered), to change. Sending it turns their offer down once the server has mine |
| Offers list | Mine first, then a ruled-off list of the others' | One list of compact cards with the two sides next to each other: for me (answers in place), mine ("waiting", WITHDRAW), between others (read-only, "Compete" TO each side). Answered offers leave the one-line trace in Recent trades |
| Competing offer | Opened the sheet prefilled | Opens the composer prefilled, titled "Competing offer" |

No pop-up opens anywhere in trading and nothing has to be closed. No box scrolls inside the
page. Chains are in the ruleset's order in the table, the chips, the lines of the deal and the
offer cards.

### The preview route

`POST /api/room/{room}/trade-preview/{player}` (read-only): the fields of a `propose_trade`
action for a draft, or `{"trade_id"}` for an open offer. Response (`game.trade_value.
trade_preview`): `valid` / `error`, both sides at list prices, `positions` (every player whose
standing changes: `position_changes`), and `bot` only for a draft addressed to a rules-based
seat (`{acceptable, cash_needed}`).

- **Same code path.** A draft's verdict is `validate_action` (what `apply_action` runs:
  `game.decisions.draft_trade`). The bot's answer is the `trade_valuation(...)["acceptable"]`
  the seat itself answers with (`HeuristicAgent._trade_response`); `cash_to_accept` finds the
  least extra cash by bisection over the same function.
- **Public information only**: holdings, cash, prices, the board. No hands, no model output.
- **Is telling the player the bot's answer acceptable game-wise?** The rule is deterministic and
  published (`docs/ai/bot-trading.md`); an offer costs nothing to make, so a player could already
  find the bot's price by proposing and re-proposing. The preview saves the taps; it reveals
  nothing new. It is never given for an LLM or a human seat. (Owner's call: see below.)

## Left as it was, and why

- **Toasts** for trade results ("Offer sent to Bob", "Bob accepted your offer"). They are not
  dialogs and news must reach a player on another tab. On the Trades tab they repeat what the
  list shows; see the matters of taste.
- **The players table on the Board tab is not a way into the composer.** Its cells are 20px
  squares sized so the board fits (BL-014); making them buttons would either miss the 44px
  target or cost the board its height. The Trades tab's own table has the room.
- **Offers between other players get no "what it would do" line**: only offers I can act on.
- **`Modal`'s sheet variant** stays in the component (unused now) with its tests.
- **The heuristic bot still never proposes trades**, so a counter-offer only comes from a human
  or an LLM seat.

## Matters of taste for the owner

1. **Showing what a rules bot would answer.** Recommendation: keep (reasoning above). To turn it
   off, drop `rules_bots` in `api/rooms.py: trade_preview_route`; the effects stay.
2. **COUNTER turns their offer down** when mine is on the table (instead of leaving both open).
   Recommendation: keep; one deal between two players at a time is easier to read.
3. **Order of the offers list**: offers for me first when the tab opens, new ones at the foot
   while it is open (so nothing moves under the thumb). The alternative, always re-sorting,
   would move the cards whenever an offer for me arrives.
4. **One-sided offers** (a gift, or asking for something for nothing) can be sent, as the rules
   allow; the old builder refused them. The empty side's heading has no value next to it, and a
   rules bot's verdict says what it thinks of the deal.
5. **Chips plus lines** instead of a stepper for every chain held: shorter, but a share is added
   by a tap on its chip rather than on a "+" in its own row.
6. **Toasts on the Trades tab** could be dropped where the list already says the same.

## Implementation Notes

- `lib/gameTabs.ts: isBusyTrading`, `hooks/useGameTabs.ts` (`isBusy`), `GameLayout.tsx` (the
  scroll effect; `openTab` so COUNTER in the table panel can open Trades by the player's own tap).
- `components/game/TradeStart.tsx` (new), `TradeBuilder.tsx` (rewritten; keeps its name and
  `trade-builder`), `TradeOffers.tsx` (one list; `IncomingOffer` with the three answers),
  `hooks/useTradePreview.ts` (asks 150 ms after the last change; the last answer stays on screen,
  dimmed, until the next one), `lib/trades.ts` (`startOffer`, `counterOffer`, `positionLines`,
  `fetchTradePreview`), `pages/player/usePlayerTrades.ts` (a counter rejects the offer it
  answers once `trade_proposed` confirms mine).
- Backend: `game/trade_value.py` (`position_changes`, `cash_to_accept`, `trade_preview`),
  `game/decisions.py: draft_trade`, `api/rooms.py: trade_preview_route`. An offer that is no
  longer open previews as `{valid: false, error: "Trade not found"}` (a page may still show it
  for a moment), not as a 404.
- Screen tests can seat the real rules-based bot (`startFromSnapshot(..., { bots: ['p4'] })`).

### Deviations from the brief
- **"Offers list: mine, for me, between others"**: offers for me come first, and only when the
  tab opens; while it is open the order does not change (cause 4 above).
- **Quick cash buttons "+$500 / +$1,000"** were left out: − / + in $100 steps, a typed amount,
  EVEN AT LIST and the bot's ADD $X cover the amounts a player reaches for, in less room.
- **A draft valuation in `game_state`** was not added: the preview is asked over HTTP when the
  draft changes, so nothing about a draft is sent to anyone else.
