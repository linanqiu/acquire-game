# BL-007: Game screens: net worth, majorities, merger result, less clutter

## Metadata
- **Epic**: Backlog
- **Status**: `completed`
- **Priority**: `high`
- **Effort**: `L` (2-4 hours)
- **Dependencies**: ES-004 (calculator pack)

## Context

Owner feedback after playing a few rounds (2026-11-15): "The interface is cluttered. But also
doesn't show important info like total wealth as of this point, and whose majority vs minority in
a hotel, and during takeovers what the result is etc."

Screenshot review of the player and host pages at 1366x768, 1280x900, 768x1024 and 390x844 in the
place-tile, buy, survivor-choice, disposition, waiting and game-over states found:

**Clutter**
- Every number shown two or three times: my cash in the header, in my players-panel row and as the
  Portfolio total; my shares in my players-panel row and again in a full-width PORTFOLIO table
  (`qty x price = value`, five columns for three facts).
- An always-open TRADES card with "No pending offers" above the players.
- The tile rack was a full-width sticky footer: with dead tiles it grew to four lines of identical
  "can't be played: it would start an 8th hotel chain" notes and covered the bottom of the side
  pane (the CONFIRM DISPOSITION button was under it at 1366x768).
- Host: 24px "SCOREBOARD" / "HOTEL CHAINS" headings, 18px body, the chains list cut off inside its
  own scroll box at 1366x768, and an "ACTIVITY: Waiting for players..." bar that never changed.
- Chrome: a 48px header row and padded phase bar; on phones the header stacked into two rows.

**Missing**
- Net worth (cash + shares at current prices) and rank, for anyone.
- Who holds the majority/minority of a chain, by how much, what it would pay, and where I stand.
- The player page did not show chain sizes, prices or SAFE at all outside the buy panel.
- Any record of a merger: bonuses were paid silently (cash changed), nobody saw who sold, traded or
  kept, and the host only showed "X'S TURN - MERGER".

## Implementation Notes

- The first commit of this work is labelled "UI-001" (renamed to BL-007 to follow the backlog
  numbering).
- Rank is by net worth (cash + shares at current prices), not by projected final score (which
  adds the bonuses still to be paid); the chains table shows those bonuses separately.
- A merger card opens by itself only for a merger that appears while the page is open (or is still
  in progress on load); mergers finished before a reload stay in the log. Closed cards are
  remembered per tab (sessionStorage).
