# BL-013: Phone UX pass: players matrix, decisions under the board, nothing to close, fewer words

## Metadata
- **Epic**: Backlog
- **Status**: `completed`
- **Priority**: `high`
- **Effort**: `L` (3+ hours)
- **Dependencies**: BL-008 (game-page tabs), BL-011 (decision tab), BL-012 (public trade offers)

## Context

Owner requests (2026-11-16), playing on an iPhone (about 390x844) against AI seats:

- "Continue thinking of aesthetics changes and improving UX/UI."
- "Is it possible to make the game UI less text dense but still just as informative?"
- "In the player-by-player tally of assets the hotel chains are in different orders. Makes
  comparison hard. Maybe a table or something like that?"
- "I find myself constantly toggling between player and board. The now tab is kind of weird and
  the merger modal requires manual closing. Close doesn't even make sense. It should just be
  there for the merger and go away after."
- The trade builder on a phone: a long form in a modal, partner cards that wrap, hints that wrap
  onto two lines, the buttons at the very bottom next to the browser toolbar.

Before: six tabs on a phone (Board, Now, Players, Chains, Chat, Trades). A decision sent the
page to the Now tab, away from the board. Each player's shares were a row of chips that skipped
the chains the player did not hold, so the same chain sat in a different place in every row. A
finished merger stayed in the Now tab until CLOSE was pressed.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] **Players matrix** (`Standings`, a real `<table>` with column and row headers): one row
      per player (seat order, me marked YOU, the player on turn marked), one column per chain in
      the ruleset's order, the same for every player. A cell is the share count: filled with the
      chain colour for the majority holder, outlined for the minority holder, `=` in front when
      the place is tied (roles from the server's `chain_holders`), a quiet dot for none. Then
      cash with shares at current prices under it, and net worth with the rank under it. Footer
      rows: tiles, price and bank stock per chain. Chains not on the board keep their column,
      dimmed. No number shown before is lost.
- [x] **It fits a phone**: 360, 390 and 1366 wide without sideways scrolling (six players).
- [x] **One chain order everywhere**: the matrix, the Chains tab (was: biggest first), the buy
      rows, the trade builder (both sides and the summary), held off-board chains.
- [x] **Standings with the board**: on a phone the Board tab shows the whole board and, under
      it, the table panel with the matrix. The Players tab is gone (phones and wide screens).
      On wide screens the table panel is the first sidebar tab, next to the board.
- [x] **Decisions in place**: there is no Now tab. A decision (buy, sell / trade / keep, found,
      survivor, a trade offer for me) appears at the top of the table panel, with the facts it
      had before, and goes when it is made. The page still brings it into view by itself (from
      another tab, once per event, never while I type in the chat; a dot on the Board tab then).
      On a short phone (360x640) the board gets smaller for the decision but stays whole, and
      the rack gives its row to the decision while it is open. No pop-up covers the board.
- [x] **Nothing to close**: a merger is shown while it runs; its result stays until the next
      tile is placed (or a decision of mine takes its place) and then goes by itself. Every
      merger remains in a quiet line ("Last merger: Marlowe ← Halcyon", and the merger log in
      the Trades tab / host Log) that opens in place and closes when tapped again. The
      welcome-back card after a reconnect goes by itself after six seconds.
- [x] **Fewer words, same information**: the status line is not repeated on the Board tab (the
      phase bar says it; it stays on the other tabs as the way back, and carries PLACE TILE once
      a tile is picked); instructions that are obvious after the first time (`ui/Hint`: placing,
      founding, survivor, "offers are public") are shown until the player has done the thing,
      then become a "?" that brings them back; table captions are for screen readers only;
      merger lines are figures ("Alice placed 6C · Marlowe 15 → 20 tiles", "1st $7,000 · 2nd
      $3,500"); the Chains tab has one header (Tiles / Price / Bank) over aligned numbers; ties
      and sole holders are "=1st … each" and "1st+2nd" instead of sentences.
- [x] **Trade builder on phones**: a full-screen sheet (`Modal variant="sheet"`, `100dvh`), one
      header, partners on one line, one line per row ("of 11" in place of "(you have 11)"),
      steppers a thumb can hit, and a footer that is always in view: "You give 2 Sandpiper
      $400 / You get $1,200 $1,200" (values at current prices), the refusal reason, CANCEL and
      PROPOSE TRADE (44px). On wide screens a taller dialog with the same footer.
- [x] **Small things**: the header numbers stay on one line whatever their size (they wrapped
      at $12,500 / $24,600, which moved the page); the count on a bottom-bar tab sits in its
      corner instead of squeezing the label ("TRAD…"); the winner's crown emoji is a plain star.
- [x] **Motion** stays short and functional (the sheet slides 16px; nothing new moves in the
      game), and is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- [x] **Tests**: unit tests updated to the new behaviour; tier 3: `table-view.spec.ts` (the
      matrix at 360 / 390 / 1366: order, markers against the fixture's holders, footer, no
      sideways scroll; the merger log in place), `tabs.spec.ts` (rewritten: four tabs, board +
      decision + rack fit in every state at four sizes), `decision-tab.spec.ts` (decisions under
      the board; the merger result shown, not closable, gone after the next tile, at 390 and
      360), `trades.spec.ts` (the sheet at 390 and 360; the hint); tier 4 journeys follow the
      new flow with UI only. Surface coverage map updated.

## Implementation Notes

- **`GameLayout`** takes `table` (the table panel) instead of `decision`. Phones: the Board tab
  is `board + table panel`; the tab is a size container and the board's `--board-max-height` is
  `100cqh - --under-min`, so the panel always keeps some height and the board shrinks rather
  than being cut. Wide: the panel is the tab `now` (label "Table"; "Players" on the host).
  `useGameTabs` takes `decisionTab` (`board` on phones, `now` on wide screens, none when the
  panel is pinned on the host's TV).
- **`DecisionTab`** is the table panel of the player page: offer, decision (or merger, or who
  the table waits for), `Standings`, the last merger line. A decision with a pinned footer is
  `min-height: 100cqh` of the panel, so its button is at the bottom of what is visible and the
  matrix is one scroll away.
- **`useMergerResult(mergers, tilesOnBoard)`** returns `auto` (the merger to show now) and
  `live`. No storage: a merger that was finished when the page loaded is never shown by itself.
- **`MergerLog`** is a list of disclosures (`aria-expanded`), each opening to `MergerResult`.
- **Hints** are remembered in `localStorage` (`buyout-hints-seen`); `markHintSeen` is called
  when the player places a tile, founds, picks a survivor or opens the trade builder.

### Deviations from the briefs
- **Rows stay in seat order** (not "me first"): rows that never move are easier to read turn
  after turn; my row is marked.
- **Cash and share value share a cell** (cash over "+ shares"), and rank sits under net worth:
  that is what makes seven chain columns and all three money figures fit 360px with no toggle.
- **"Result stays until the next tile"** rather than "until the next turn": with AI seats the
  merging player's buy takes a moment and the result would flash by.
- **The merger disposition footer** ("SELL 0 (+$0) | TRADE 0 … | HOLD 13") and "(price at
  merger)" were kept as they are.
- **Chat note and buy rows** were left alone (see follow-ups).
