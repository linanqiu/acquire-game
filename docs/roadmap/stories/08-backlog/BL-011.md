# BL-011: Decision tab: each decision with what is needed to make it; redirect instead of pop-ups

## Metadata
- **Epic**: Backlog
- **Status**: `completed`
- **Priority**: `high`
- **Effort**: `L` (2-4 hours)
- **Dependencies**: BL-007 (table view, merger record), BL-008 (game-page tabs)

## Context

Owner feedback (plays on a phone, 2026-11-15): "In the middle of M&A the windows are very crowded
and the pop-ups don't really work. Maybe make it an additional tab, because scrolling to try and
read the other players' stats is extremely cluttered. Similarly for buying shares: people often
want a reference of how many shares the other players have. In general I don't think the pop-ups
work; you probably have to make separate tabs and then redirect us to the tabs. Show relevant
information together." Added during the work: "during M&A you should show the post-M&A number of
shares in a hotel chain so that people can make decisions rationally."

Before (screenshots at 390x844, 360x640, 1366x768): during a merger the `MergerResult` card sat
on top of whatever tab was open and the disposition form filled the dock, so on a 390x844 phone
the board was a strip and the merger card about 110px; who held what was on other tabs. The buy
card showed price and bank stock but nobody's holdings.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] **Decision tab "Now"**, always present in a game: first in the wide sidebar, after Board on
      phones (six tabs fit 360px). Views (`decision-view[data-view]`): `buy`, `disposition`,
      `merger` (someone else deciding, read-only), `survivor`, `found`, `place`, `waiting`,
      `merger-result` (opened from the log). Incoming trade offers are shown first in every view.
- [x] **Buying:** one row per chain on the board: marker, price, size, bank stock, the holders in
      rank order with the engine's 1st / 2nd marks and ties (`HolderChips`), me always (0 when I
      hold none), the 1st / 2nd bonus now, the stepper, and how my purchase moves me ("takes 2nd",
      "would tie 1st"; share counts only). Total, cash after and the buttons are pinned at the
      bottom of the tab: on screen without scrolling at 390x844 with seven chains, and at 360x640.
- [x] **Merger in one view:** takeover, sizes, each defunct holder with shares, the bonus the
      engine paid and their decision (sold / traded / kept, deciding, to decide, to decide later
      for a queued chain); the **survivor after the merger** (engine size, price, 1st / 2nd bonus
      at that size, bank stock, everyone's survivor shares with trades already made, undecided
      players); when it is my decision, my sell / trade / keep controls with cash after and
      survivor shares after, and my survivor row, place and the bank following the trade slider
      live. CONFIRM is pinned at the bottom.
- [x] **Survivor choice** with each tied chain's size, price, bank and holders; **founding** with
      the tiers' starting prices and the founder's free share; **place tile** points to the Board
      with the rack notes (phones); **waiting** says who the table waits on and for what, with the
      last merger result (closable) or a "Last merger" link.
- [x] **Redirect instead of pop-ups:** a new event (my survivor / founding / buy / disposition
      decision, an offer for me, a merger starting or resolving) opens the decision tab once per
      event; never while I type in the chat (focus or draft): a dot badge on Now instead. My
      place-tile turn still opens the Board on phones (a badge on Now for anything arriving with
      it). After a reload the remembered tab stays and what is pending gets the badge. The
      mergers log opens a merger in the decision tab.
- [x] **Phone dock** is one status line (`status-line`: "Your move: buy shares", "Waiting for
      Lulu to dispose of their shares"; taps open Now; PLACE TILE / END GAME on it while placing)
      plus the rack. No action card, no merger card over tabs; the Board tab shows the full board
      (every square inside the viewport) in every phase at 390x844 and 360x640.
- [x] **Wide screens:** board, status line and rack on the left; Now is the first sidebar tab

- [x] **Host:** a Now tab with who the table waits on and the merger in progress (read-only, with
      the survivor after the merger) or the last result; the Log reopens a merger there.
- [x] **Backend:** each merger record carries `survivor_after` (see Implementation Notes),
      documented in `docs/architecture/seat-contract.md`, typed in `frontend/src/types/api.ts`;

- [x] **Tests:** tier 1 `test_public_table.py` (`test_survivor_after_the_merger`,

## Implementation Notes

- `survivor_after` (`game/calculator.py: _survivor_after`): `size` comes from
  `Board.absorb_merger` run on a copy of the board; `absorb_merger` is now the engine's own
  finalisation (`MergerMixin._finalize_merger` calls it), so the projection and the real merger
  cannot disagree. `price` / bonuses are the hotel's for that size; holders are today's survivor
  shares (trades already made included, `received` says how many) plus, with 0 shares, players
  still holding defunct shares (`undecided`). Roles and amounts are `Rules.calculate_bonuses` at
  the post-merger size with today's holdings. `null` once the merger is complete; the finished
  merger view shows the survivor's current holders from `chain_holders` instead.
- The frontend only compares share counts (`lib/standing.ts`: place, tie, gap, "takes 2nd"); every
  amount is the server's.
- Events and views are pure functions (`lib/decision.ts`); `useGameTabs` keeps the seen events
  and the badge; `GameLayout` adds the Now tab and the status line slot; `openNow` lets the
  mergers log open the tab.
- The confirm button and the buy footer are `position: sticky` inside the view's root, so they
  stay on screen while the context scrolls inside the tab.

### Deviations from the brief
- **Label "Now"**: shorter than "Move" and true in every state (waiting, host).
- **Wide screens: a tab, not pinned.** At 1366x768 the disposition view (controls, defunct holders,
  survivor after) is taller than the sidebar, so per the brief it is the first tab, not pinned.
- **Bonuses in the holders' rows**: the separate "Bonuses paid" table was merged into the defunct
  holders table (one row per holder: shares, bonus paid, decision) to save four rows on phones.
- **Buy replaces a finished merger** (as the brief says); a "Last merger" link reopens it, and
  BACK returns to the buy.
- **Trades-tab dot for a new merger removed**: the Now badge does that job now.
- The status line keeps PLACE TILE / END GAME while placing a tile (the Board tab is where you
  place, and END GAME must not hide in another tab).

## Follow-ups (not done)
- At 360x640 the disposition view shows the controls and CONFIRM; the holders and the survivor
  table need a scroll inside the tab (the page itself does not scroll).
- In a multi-chain merger the second defunct chain's holders are listed "to decide later" with
  today's counts; the projection does not model the second chain's bonus order beyond that.
