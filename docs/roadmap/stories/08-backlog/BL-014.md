# BL-014: The Board tab fits a real phone screen; a calmer theme

## Metadata
- **Epic**: Backlog
- **Status**: `completed`
- **Priority**: `high`
- **Effort**: `L` (3+ hours)
- **Dependencies**: BL-013 (phone UX pass), BL-009 (visual pass), BL-010 (seat badge)

## Context

Owner feedback (2026-11-16) on BL-013, with a screenshot from an iPhone (Chrome, dark theme,
the start of a four-player game against bots):

- "The table still requires scrolling up and down."
- "The bot icon is unnecessarily big."
- "Also freshen up the entire theme a little, not sure [about] this."

What the screenshot showed. The page area a phone browser really leaves with its toolbars
showing is about **390x675** CSS px, not the 844 of the device that BL-013 was checked at. In it
the players matrix needed 269px and had 196: the bank row was cut off and the panel scrolled.
Every player row was two lines (a truncated name over a 32px round icon; cash over "+$0"; worth
over "=#1"), a sentence of instructions took a line of its own, and the footer printed three
rows of dots because no chain was on the board yet.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] **Nothing scrolls at 390x675 and 375x630 with four players**: header, phase bar, the
      whole board, every player row, the footer (tiles / price / bank) and the rack are on
      screen, nothing cut, at the start of a game and in the middle of one.
- [x] **Six players at 390x675** fit as well (all six rows, the footer, the "last merger"
      line).
- [x] **360x600 degrades in one way**: four players fit; with six the board stays whole and the
      table panel scrolls inside itself (about 50px; four rows show). Nothing else scrolls and
      nothing is wider than the screen.
- [x] **One line per player** (28px): name, seat-kind icon, YOU; share counts; cash; net worth
      with the rank in front (`#1`, `=#1`). AI seats print their first name where the column is
      narrow ("Dolly" for "Dolly Dollars", only if that is still unique at the table; a person's
      name is never cut); the whole name is in the DOM, the tooltip and the popover.
- [x] **Small seat icon**: a bare 14px icon in the name's line. It is still a button with a
      32px hit area that opens the popover, which now also gives the seat's cash and share value.
- [x] **The matrix is never wider than its panel**: fixed layout, no clipped right edge, no
      scrollbar over the numbers.
- [x] **No line of instructions**: how to place a tile, and the matrix's legend, are behind a
      "?" at the end of the phase bar (accent-coloured until the first tile is placed).
- [x] **The footer is left out while no chain is on the board**; with chains it is three 15px

- [x] **Theme**: neutral ink surfaces instead of navy, one accent used for my turn / my

## Implementation Notes

- **The board yields exactly what the table needs.** `GameLayout` measures the height of the
  table panel's content (a `ResizeObserver` on an inner wrapper whose height does not depend on
  the panel) and hands it to the CSS as `--table-need`. `.boardTab` turns it into
  `--board-max-height`, as before. New in `Board.module.css`: a square is `--cell` wide and
  `--cell-h` tall. A page may allow squares wider than tall (`--cell-stretch: 1.3` on the
  phone's Board tab), so a board short of height keeps its full width and only gets flatter,
  down to `--cell-floor` (20px); only then does the panel scroll. While a decision is open the
  panel still gets its fixed share (`min(330px, 58cqh)`), but the board now keeps its width
  there too (29 x 23px squares at 390x675, where it used to shrink to 22.6px squares and a board
narrower than the screen).
- **`Standings`**: one line per row; `lib/shortNames.ts` decides what a narrow row prints;
  `data-footer` says whether the footer is drawn. The legend is `StandingsLegend`, shown by
  `PhaseHelp`.
- **`PhaseHelp`** (`ui/`): the "?" button and its panel. It sits next to the status region,
  not in it, so opening it announces nothing. `PageShell` takes `help` and `helpFresh`.
- **`SeatKindBadge`** takes `size="inline"` and `extra` (a line for the popover).
- **`Button`** has an `accent` variant: PLACE TILE, BUY / SKIP & END TURN, CONFIRM DISPOSITION,
  START GAME. **`Tile`** takes `picked` (the board square I chose is drawn in the accent).
- **Theme** is `styles/tokens.css` plus small edits where a component drew its own outline:

  New: `--playable-bg` / `--playable-border` (a square I can play: a quiet fill and a 1px ring),
  `--tile-bg` (a rack tile: a raised neutral chip), `--font-num` (the UI face; the body sets

  around the header, the tab bar, the rack, the sidebar, cards and the status line; the white
  outlines on the disposition card, my holder chip and my buy rows; the accent edges on the
  phase bar; uppercase on tabs, merger titles, tier labels. `--font-mono` remains for the room
  code and raw transcript text only.

- **Contrast** (WCAG, computed from the tokens): text on every surface is AA or better in both
  themes. Lowest pairs, dark: secondary text on a rack tile 5.4, on the turn tint 6.0; negative
  on a rack tile 5.7. Light: gold on the turn tint 5.0, white on the orphan tile 5.0, the
  accent on the turn tint (the phase bar on my turn) 5.1, secondary text on the turn tint 5.2,
  white on the accent button 6.4. Chain labels (dark `#111` on
  the ruleset colours) 6.7 to 9.8, unchanged. Coordinates on empty squares are faint on purpose
  (2.7 dark, 2.4 light): they are also in the tile's accessible name and the rack. Non-text:
  chain fills against an empty square are 6.0+ in the dark theme and 1.9 to 2.7 in the light
  one (as before this story; the two-letter label is what identifies a chain).

### Deviations from the brief
- **Chain colours were not changed.** They are ruleset data (`backend/rulesets/*.json`, with a
  frozen-colour test and generated fixtures), this story is frontend-only, and BL-009 already
  tuned them (similar OKLab lightness, colour-vision distances asserted in
  `tests/unit/themes.test.tsx`). On neutral ink they read calmer than on navy.
- **Button labels stay in capitals.** They are literal strings that many tests find by
  name; the tracking was reduced instead. Capitals went from tabs and titles.
- **The footer stays three rows** (tiles, price, bank) rather than a merged "price · bank"
  row: two numbers do not fit a 25px column. It costs 47px and is hidden while it would be
  empty.
- **"+$ shares" for a human opponent on a phone** is only in the cells' tooltip (worth minus
  cash); AI seats have it in the popover, and wide panels print it.
- **Squares may go down to 20px tall** (not 28) before the panel scrolls: at 390 wide a square
  is 28.4px to begin with, so a 28px floor would have left nothing to give. They are full
  squares whenever four players are at the table and no extra line is showing.

### The other direction (not built)
A warmer "table" theme: a deep green-grey felt for the board bed, cream tiles with an engraved
coordinate, a serif brand mark and a brass accent. It would give the game more character and
separate the accent from the two blue chains, but it is a new identity rather than a refresh,
it fights the seven chain colours for attention (they are tuned for a neutral ground), and the
light theme would need its own design. The neutral ink direction keeps the app recognisable and
lets the board's colours be the only saturated thing on screen.
