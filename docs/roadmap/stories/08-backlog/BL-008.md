## Implementation Notes

- `GameLayout` (`src/components/game/GameLayout.tsx`) holds the layout logic for both pages; the
  pages only pass the board, rack, "now" content, merger card and tab contents. `useGameTabs`
  (`src/hooks/useGameTabs.ts`) holds the tab state; the rules are pure functions in
  `src/lib/gameTabs.ts`.
- In game, `PageShell` runs in `fill` mode: `position: fixed; inset: 0; height: 100dvh`, which
  leaves `#root`'s max width and padding (so a TV gets the whole width) and makes sure the page
  itself never scrolls.
- The board fits both the width and the height of its box: the box is a size container and
  `--board-max-height: 100cqh`. The player page now uses the `lg` board (squares up to 64px).
- Every tab panel stays mounted (`hidden` when not selected) so a chat draft and scroll positions
  survive a switch.

### Deviations from the brief
- Phones: the "now" area (action card, offers for me, rack) sits at the **bottom**, right above the
  tab bar, instead of at the top: that is where the thumb is, and it keeps the action, the rack and
  the tabs together. The phase line stays at the top (in the shell). The tab content fills the
  space between the phase line and the dock.
- The fifth player tab is labelled **Trades** (not "Trades & log"): five labels must fit 360px. It
  holds the mergers log too.
- Offers waiting for my answer moved out of the Trades tab into the "now" area (they are a
  decision). `TradeOffers` takes `part="incoming" | "mine"`; the incoming part has the test id
  `trade-incoming`.
- Decision cards are more compact on phones (smaller card padding and gaps), and the END TURN and
  CONFIRM DISPOSITION buttons stick to the bottom of the action card, so on a 360x640 phone, where
  the buy card is taller than its space, the button stays in view while the chain list scrolls.
- The chat badge counts only lines said after a first visit: the history sent on connect counts
  as read (store field `chatHistoryId`); a reload keeps its own mark.
- Tablets (768x1024 portrait) get the wide layout as the brief asks; the board is about 430px wide
  there and leaves empty space under it on the left (see follow-ups).

## Follow-ups (not done)

  top, sidebar below) may use the height better. Not tested on a real tablet.
- Phone keyboard: the layout uses `100dvh`; when the on-screen keyboard opens over the Chat tab the
  behaviour depends on the browser (not testable in headless Chromium).
- At 360x640 the decision cards (buy with 5 chains, disposition) scroll inside the dock and the tab
  content above shrinks to about 100px; usable, but tight.
