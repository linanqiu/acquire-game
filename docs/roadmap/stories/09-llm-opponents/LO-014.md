## Context

Proposal (2026-11-21): "Let the AIs play each other live, with commentary. The watch page
already exists. Add a commentator model narrating like a sports broadcast, and it becomes
something you'd leave on a second screen." Owner: "Commentary is a good idea."

What it is, when it speaks, what it is told, what it costs and what went wrong on the way:
[docs/ai/commentary.md](../../../ai/commentary.md). Wire format and transcript lines:
[seat-contract.md, "Commentary"](../../../architecture/seat-contract.md).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] **A commentator that is not a seat** (`backend/commentary.py`,
      `backend/agents/llm/commentary.py`): never goes through `apply_action`, never changes the
      game, sees public information only.
- [x] **When it speaks is decided by rules**: a chain founded, a merger (who collected what), a
      majority changing hands (when it is worth a line), a trade accepted or rejected, a chain
      going safe, a new leader in net worth, the end becoming possible, the final result; a
      "state of the table" line after 8 quiet turns. Moments are batched: at most one line per
      `commentary_min_gap_s` (8 s), at most 80 lines a game; nothing notable, no line.
- [x] **What it says**: one or two short sentences from computed facts (bonuses if a chain were
      acquired now, who is ahead and by how much, what a merger paid, how many mergers a player
      has set off), with its own last six lines against repetition and for callbacks. Anything
      not in the prompt is forbidden; an answer with an amount of money that is not in the
      prompt, an exclamation mark, an emoji, a hype word or a repeated sentence is refused and
      the rules line is shown instead.
- [x] **Cost control**: only while commentary is on for the room and a spectator is connected;
      one small call per line with a cached static system prompt, low effort and a small output
      cap; `GAME_COMMENTARY_MODEL` (unset: the catalog's Haiku entry when it has a key, else
      rules-only; never another model unless named); caps of 60 calls and $0.05 per game; calls
      in the transcript with tokens and cost; a rules-only fallback (templated lines from the
      same facts) with no model, past a cap or after a failure; never blocks or delays the game.
- [x] **Delivery**: `commentary` to the host/watch connection, `commentary_history` on connect
      and when the setting changes; every line in the transcript (`commentary` lines, a
      `commentary` block in the footer).
- [x] **Watch page**: a strip with the latest line large (readable across a room on a TV) and
      the two before it quieter, auto-advancing, fixed in height so the board does not move; one
      compact line above the tab bar on phones. A switch for the room (OFF in the strip; TURN ON
      in the Log tab; off stops the model calls). `rules` / `model` said quietly. Earlier lines
      are in the Log tab; each line is on the page once; nothing scrolls inside the page.
      Players' own pages have no commentary.
- [x] **"Watch bots play" is a show**: one click starts a game of AI players with commentary
      (`POST /create-show`) and lands on the watch page; the game starts when the page
      connects; a watched all-AI game is played at the broadcast pace (1.2 s / 0.7 s); at the
      end the commentator's last line stands over the standings, with WATCH ANOTHER. The host
      lobby (pick your own AI players) is reached by "Choose the players of a new game".
- [x] **Transcript viewer**: commentary lines at their steps, quiet.
- [x] Tier 1 `backend/tests/test_commentary.py` (18), tier 2
      `backend/tests/protocol/test_commentary.py` (17), unit
      `frontend/src/components/game/Commentary.test.tsx` (18), tier 3
      `frontend/tests/e2e/screens/commentary.spec.ts` (3), tier 4 the spectator journey extended
      from the home page. Surface map: HO-14..HO-18, TX-14b, H-03.
- [x] Real-model sample, Claude Haiku 5.5 only: eight rounds of bot games (19 in all) read line by line;
      what was wrong and how it was fixed is in docs/ai/commentary.md. About half a cent a game.
- [x] Fixtures unchanged (`python -m fixtures_gen`).

## Decisions and deviations

- **No commentary on players' pages, and no player switch.** A player has the table, the chat
  and their own decisions on a phone; the commentary is for whoever watches. The host page of a
  game with humans in it can turn it on (Log tab).
- **The Log tab keeps its mergers and trades.** The commentary line says a merger in a sentence;
  the Log's merger line opens to the full payout table and its trade lines carry the terms:
  reference, not narration. What moved into the Log is the commentary's own history (the lines
  no longer in the strip), so each commentary line is on the page once.
- **A show starts when its first spectator connects**, not when the room is created: at the
  fast pace ten turns would be gone before the page had opened its socket, and a game nobody
  watches would be narrated for nobody.
- **The host socket is cleared when it closes** (`SessionManager.disconnect_host`). Before, it
  stayed set until a send failed; "a spectator is connected" has to be true only while one is.
- **Foundings of a chain that was acquired before are a line only when someone kept its
  shares**; majority changes only when 5+ shares and a $3,000+ bonus are at stake, once per
  chain in 6 turns. Without these the commentary of a bot game was mostly "X takes the Y
  majority".
- **Effort stays low.** One round at medium: twice the cost, about 4 s per line, and more
  conclusions of its own.

## Known limits

- One host/watch connection per room (as before): a second viewer of the same room takes the
  socket over and the first page stops updating. A show per viewer ("Watch bots play") is the
  way to watch; sharing one room between screens needs a list of host sockets (follow-up).
- Claude Haiku 5.5 at low effort still pads now and then with a clause the facts do not carry
  (see "Still seen" in docs/ai/commentary.md). Amounts of money cannot be wrong (checked against
  the prompt); other words can.
- A host-forced end of game closes the transcript at once: its final line is shown but, when it
  comes from the model, not written to the transcript.
- The seats of a show are fixed by `SHOW_SEATS` (default four rules-based bots). LLM seats in
  it cost their usual price on every show anyone starts.
